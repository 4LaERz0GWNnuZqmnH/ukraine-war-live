Support this project

Ukraine War Live is a personal, non-commercial project. It is free to use, carries no ads and no trackers, and nothing is behind a paywall.

Running it still costs a little real money. Contributions go toward exactly two things:

  • the bugg.club domain registration — about $15 a year (as of August 2026);
  • the Claude subscription (Anthropic) used to build, maintain, and keep the site running — part of the ongoing upkeep.

That is the whole list. Anything given beyond those costs just reduces what the next year takes to keep online. Donations are optional and unlock nothing extra — the site stays exactly the same for everyone.

Prefer to help Ukraine directly? That matters far more than this site does. A few established options, by area:

  • UNITED24 — Ukraine's official state fundraising platform; you pick the cause (defence, demining, medical aid, or rebuilding), with public reporting.
  • White Stork — US veteran-led 501(c)(3) delivering battlefield first-aid kits (IFAKs) and trauma supplies to front-line soldiers and civilians.
  • Razom for Ukraine — US non-profit running emergency response, field medicine, and community relief through local partners.
  • The HALO Trust — landmine and unexploded-ordnance clearance in liberated areas.

Listed for convenience, not as endorsement — check any charity yourself before giving. This site takes no cut and has no affiliation with them.
